FNaF 3 Redone for AYN Thor is an unofficial launcher and companion for the Steam release of Five Nights at Freddy's 3 (build 1892839). It contains no game files. The player imports their own FiveNightsatFreddys3.exe, which runs unmodified through the bundled compatibility runtime.
